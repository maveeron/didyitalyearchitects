About Me

Design intent, connected to coordinated information.

I am Arch. Ma. Veronica B. Manuel, a registered architect with a Master’s degree in Architecture, specializing in architectural design, BIM management, digital delivery, and project quality assurance.

My professional background combines design thinking, technical documentation, BIM implementation, project coordination, and information management. I have experience supporting local and international clients, developing digital workflows, and promoting consistent standards across architectural projects.
